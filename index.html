About

My research centres on natural language processing and large language models, with a particular focus on their evaluation: cultural knowledge, reasoning, and behaviour in low-resource languages such as Persian.

I am also interested in trustworthy language models — interpretability, alignment, and safety — as well as controllable text generation, creativity in AI, and multimodal and agentic NLP systems.

Previously, I spent three years at the IUST NLP Laboratory building Persian benchmarks and multimodal systems — work that led to first-place results in the ImageCLEFmedical Caption shared task.
